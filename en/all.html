It's not about you. What people pay for is changing

A junior with AI covers tasks from below. A senior covers them from above — by saying "we're solving the wrong problem." The middle developer ends up in between: writes good code, reliable, knows the codebase. But all of that is execution. And execution is getting cheaper faster than you can reach your next performance review.

Middle developers are vulnerable not because they work badly. They're vulnerable because they work well in exactly the layer that's losing value. It's not a sentence. It's a diagnosis.— Illya Klymov
