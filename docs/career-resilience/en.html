# 02 · self-check

Your position may be more vulnerable than it looks

The idea for this session came after a career consultation with a strong, high-performing specialist who had been laid off.Why wasn't doing good work enough?

The session fits developers across stacks. The best time for a check-up is while everything is still going well.

  • —You close tasks well but rarely take part in decisions
  • —You can list what you've done, but it's hard to explain its value to the company
  • —You grow in experience and salary, but not in influence
  • —You don't know the criteria your leadership uses to judge your work
  • —You want to reach Senior or Lead, but mostly develop technical skills
  • —You see AI speeding up code and can't tell what companies will keep paying an engineer for
  • —You want to assess the risks before the situation turns critical

# 03 · result

After the session you'll be able to

01 Assess the risks of your position

You'll spot early signals in tasks, responsibility, and communication that shouldn't be ignored.

02 See whether the company recognizes your value

You'll understand what a manager evaluates beyond code quality, speed, and the number of closed tasks.

03 Separate your zone of influence from the company's decisions

You'll see what can be strengthened and what more productivity can't fix: budget, a project shutdown, or a change of strategy.

04 Choose a concrete next step

You'll decide what needs attention now: engineering thinking, AI, visibility of impact, communication, or external career options.

Your own risk map and a clear sense of what to strengthen in your position.
