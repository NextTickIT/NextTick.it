The mechanism

A recipe you can't drop a single word from.

For you to be the one chosen when it's time to decide "who to promote and who to cut," you need one thing — and it's been proven on real careers, not in theory:

regularly deliver a lot of visible results
Regularly → architecture

With AI, your productivity peaks at the start, then everything bogs down: the agent mimics the project's chaos and multiplies it. Only architecture lets you hold the pace for months.

A lot → AI-agents

The "a lot via overtime" path leads to burnout. The alternative is agents. But they deliver "a lot" only for someone who can frame a task so the agent solves it instead of guessing.

Visible → communication

You can lay down a brilliant platform and still get cut — because your manager didn't understand your value. Visibility is a skill, and it's available to introverts.

Results → solving problems

A result isn't code. A result is a solved business problem. That's exactly what gets evaluated when they decide who to keep.

What's inside

You're not signing up for a course. You're subscribing to a rhythm.

Courses can't keep up with the industry, and nobody has time for four-hour lectures — not you, not us. So the format is different: background learning built into your work week. There's no "A→B→C" sequence — you can start on any week.

01 · Three topics every week

A topic is a learning product about one thing: a compressed concept in 15 minutes plus a task for the week in your work project. One topic per track: architecture, AI-agents, communication.

02 · Workshops

The architecture workshop (led by Timur), the AI-agent-taming workshop, and the effective-communication workshop (led by Illya). Ask a question about your own case — get a breakdown.

03 · Digests

A day and week summary: 1,200 workshop messages condensed into 10 minutes of reading. Read only those — and you're still in context.

04 · Two live calls a month

With Illya and with Timur. Your work situation — anonymously, on a live call. Recordings stay in the base.

anatomy of a topic: compressed concept → key takeaways → a task in your project → exercises by difficulty level → a question to the workshop if you're stuck
First month

First results in the first week. Here's what happens over four.

Week 1 · Decision context

Video → assignment: take one architectural decision in your project that you treat as a given, and find its constraint. Result: the first decision you can explain and defend.

Week 2 · The language of consequences

Video → assignment: reframe one piece of tech debt in terms of time and money, not "the code is bad." Result: an argument your manager hears the first time.

Week 3 · The prompt as an interface to thinking

Video → assignment: one work task broken down so thoroughly that the agent has nothing to guess. Result: the task gets closed faster — and you understand why.

Week 4 · Vertical slices + status update

Video → assignment: break a 3+ day task into working pieces and write an update before anyone asks. Result: your tech lead sees someone who has the situation in hand.

From there — deeper: working with uncertainty, ADR, RFC, cross-team influence. We'll send the current month's program in the bot right after you subscribe.
