4.9. Website visitor data: cookies, web analytics data, data from the analysis of behaviour on the site and from advertising pixels (section 10 of this Policy).

4.13. Data of participants in loyalty programmes: identifiers of the persons invited and the fact that they have taken out a Subscription, the bonuses and rewards accrued.

9. RIGHTS OF THE DATA SUBJECT

9.1. In accordance with Article 8 of the Law, the data subject has the right:

  • to know about the sources of collection and the location of their personal data, the purpose of their processing, and the location of the owner (controller) or processor of personal data;
  • to receive information on the conditions for granting access to personal data, in particular information on the third parties to whom their personal data are transferred;
  • to access their personal data;
  • to receive, no later than 30 (thirty) calendar days from the day the request is received, a reply as to whether their personal data are being processed, and also to receive the content of such personal data;
  • to submit a reasoned demand to the owner (controller) of personal data objecting to the processing of their personal data;
  • to submit a reasoned demand for the amendment or destruction of their personal data if these data are processed unlawfully or are inaccurate;
  • to the protection of their personal data against unlawful processing and accidental loss, destruction or damage, and also against the provision of information that is inaccurate or that discredits the honour, dignity and business reputation of a natural person;
  • to lodge complaints about the processing of their personal data with the Ukrainian Parliament Commissioner for Human Rights or with a court;
  • to apply legal remedies in the event of a violation of the legislation on personal data protection;
  • to make reservations concerning the limitation of the right to process their personal data when giving consent;
  • to withdraw consent to the processing of personal data;
  • to know the mechanism of the automatic processing of personal data;
  • to protection against an automated decision that has legal consequences for them.

9.2. A demand for the deletion of personal data without which the provision of the Services is impossible is treated as the User's withdrawal from the Agreement under the procedure of clause 7.3.2 of the Offer (clause 6.11 of the Offer). Deletion does not extend to data that the Provider is obliged to store by law or that are necessary for the protection of its rights in a dispute (clause 8.2).

9.3. If the Provider applies automated means of detecting atypical activity (clause 4.2.8 of the Offer), their signals are used as a ground for verification and for temporary measures. The User has the right to appeal against a decision to restrict, suspend or terminate access under the procedure of section 9 of Annex 1 to the Offer. The complaint is considered by the Provider or by a person authorised by it, and not by an automated system, taking into account the explanations submitted by the User.

10.1. The website https://nexttick.it/ uses cookies and similar technologies:

  • necessary cookies — for the correct operation of the site;
  • analytics cookies (in particular Google Analytics) — for analysing traffic;
  • visitor behaviour analysis tools (in particular Microsoft Clarity) — they record clicks, scrolling and cursor movements on the pages of the site and may generate recordings of browsing sessions for the purpose of improving the site;
  • advertising cookies and pixels (in particular Meta Pixel) — for assessing the effectiveness and personalisation of advertising.

10.2. Necessary cookies are used in order to provide the functions requested by the visitor, in particular logging in to an account, the operation of the session and ensuring security. Optional analytics and advertising cookies, pixels and session recordings are permitted only after the prior voluntary consent of the visitor to the relevant purpose. The installation of a tool on the site does not in itself constitute permission for it to collect or transmit data.

10.3. Before consent is given, the visitor is provided with information on the tools actually applied, their providers, the purposes, the data and the validity periods of cookies and of data storage. Consent is recorded by a separate unambiguous action. Visiting the site, scrolling a page, accepting the Offer or paying for a Subscription do not in themselves constitute such consent. Refusal of optional technologies does not limit access to the main functions of the site or to the Services paid for.

10.4. The Provider ensures the possibility of refusing optional technologies, and also of changing the choice made or withdrawing consent by a method no more difficult than that of giving it. After withdrawal, the further collection and transmission of data by the relevant tools cease. Until a mechanism for obtaining, recording and withdrawing consent has been implemented, the launch of optional analytics and advertising technologies is not permitted.

10.5. In addition, the visitor may block or delete cookies in the browser settings and make use of the tools of the relevant providers. These possibilities do not replace the obtaining of prior consent under this Policy. Blocking necessary cookies may affect the operation of individual functions of the site. Providers of analytics and advertising services also process data in accordance with their own privacy policies.

11.2. The request should specify: the name, the identifier of the Telegram account (username or telegram_id) or of the other digital access channel through which the Subscription was taken out, and the substance of the demand.

11.3. In order to prevent unlawful access to personal data, the Provider has the right to request confirmation that the request has been received from the data subject — in particular, the sending of the request from the Telegram account to which the Subscription is linked, or the provision of information on the last payment (date, amount, last digits of the card). Identity documents are requested only in cases where the ownership of the account cannot be confirmed by another means. The request is registered on the day it is received. If additional information is required in order to verify the identity of the applicant or the applicant's ownership of the relevant account, the Provider requests it without undue delay and only to the extent necessary. Until the applicant's identity has been duly confirmed, personal data are not disclosed to the applicant. A request for additional information does not in itself suspend or restart the periods for considering the request established by law and by clause 11.4 of this Policy. If the information provided is insufficient for the request to be fulfilled, the Provider shall, within the established period, inform the applicant of the reasons and of the information necessary for its fulfilment.

11.4. Within 10 (ten) business days from the day the request is received, the Provider informs the data subject whether the request will be granted, or of the grounds on which the relevant data are not to be provided. The request is granted within 30 (thirty) calendar days from the day it is received, unless another period is established by law. Access by the data subject to data about themselves is provided free of charge.
